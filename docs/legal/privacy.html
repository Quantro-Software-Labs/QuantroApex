Privacy Policy

QuantroApex by Quantro Software Labs · Last updated: 1 October 2026

QuantroApex (“the App”) helps you simulate and, after identity verification, execute trades. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Host this page at https://apex.quantrolabs.app/privacy for store review.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: email and/or phone, display name (Firebase Auth)
  • Profile: experience level, onboarding choices, Risk Vault settings
  • Trading: orders, fills, portfolio and simulation state you generate
  • Identity (live trading only): KYC documents processed by Sumsub
  • Payments: billing email and Paystack transaction references (not full card numbers)
  • Device: push notification tokens; optional analytics events (PostHog)

2. How we use data

  • Operate simulation and live trading features you enable
  • Enforce Risk Vault limits and KYC before live orders
  • Process subscriptions and send transactional notices
  • Improve reliability (crash reporting via Sentry when configured)

3. Sharing

We share data with processors required to run the product: Firebase (auth), Supabase/Postgres (storage), Alpaca and other brokers you connect, Sumsub (KYC), Paystack (payments), and optionally PostHog/Sentry. We do not sell personal data.

4. Retention & deletion

Account and trading records are kept while your account is active. Contact support to request deletion; legal retention may apply for financial records.
